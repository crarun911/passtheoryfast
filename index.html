What Topics Are in the Theory Test?

The DVSA theory test covers 14 topic categories. Our practice questions cover all the most important ones.

🚗 Alertness & Attitude

Concentration, tiredness, drink-driving limits, mobile phone use, and driving under the influence of medication.

🔧 Safety & Your Vehicle

Tyre tread depth, tyre pressure, ABS brakes, power steering, lights and basic vehicle maintenance checks.

📏 Rules of the Road

Speed limits, stopping distances, priority at junctions, crossings, one-way streets and road positioning.

🚦 Road & Traffic Signs

Warning signs, mandatory signs, information signs, road markings and traffic light sequences.

🛣️ Motorway Rules

Smart motorways, lane discipline, emergency refuge areas, hard shoulder rules and overhead gantry signs.

🌿 Vehicle & Environment

Fuel efficiency, catalytic converters, MOT certificates, emissions and eco-friendly driving techniques.

⚠️ Incidents & Breakdowns

What to do at accidents, first aid, motorway breakdowns, aggressive drivers and fire in vehicles.

👁️ Hazard Perception

Identifying developing hazards in 14 video clips. Score up to 5 points per clip — earlier response = higher score.

Frequently Asked Questions

The UK driving theory test has 50 multiple choice questions. You have 57 minutes to complete them and must score at least 43 out of 50 to pass this section.
You need to score at least 44 out of 75 on the hazard perception section. There are 14 video clips, each worth up to 5 points. One clip contains two hazards and is worth up to 5 points each.
The multiple choice section allows 57 minutes. After a short supervised break, the hazard perception section takes approximately 20 minutes. The full test appointment is around 1 hour 30 minutes.
The official DVSA theory test costs £23 for a car driver in 2026. You can book online at gov.uk. If you fail, you must wait at least 3 working days before retaking and pay the fee again.
Click as soon as you see a developing hazard — something that would cause you to change speed or direction. The earlier you click within the scoring window, the more points you score (up to 5). Do not click repeatedly as this triggers the cheat detection and scores zero.
There is no limit to the number of times you can take the theory test. However, you must wait at least 3 working days between attempts and pay £23 each time. Once passed, your theory test certificate is valid for 2 years.
